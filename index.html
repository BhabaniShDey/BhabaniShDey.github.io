01 — Research

Is stability sufficient? Is safety sufficient? Or do we need both?

My research lies at the intersection of nonlinear control theory, robotics and safety-critical autonomous systems. I develop mathematically rigorous frameworks for stability, safety and performance of nonlinear and interconnected systems operating in uncertain environments — combining contraction theory, incremental stability, switched systems, prescribed performance control and passivity-based design, with applications in robotics, autonomous systems and human–robot collaboration.

The aim: scalable, experimentally validated control architectures for learning-enabled autonomous systems that operate safely in the real world, with formal guarantees on stability and performance.

Research areas

Area 1

Human–robot collaboration

Passivity- and impedance-based interaction control that keeps physical human–robot interaction stable and safe — staying compliant to the person while respecting limits on force and workspace, even with unknown human behaviour and learning-based adaptation.

  • Safety via reference-trajectory shaping in admittance control
  • Adaptive and variable impedance control with contraction guarantees
  • Fixed-time concurrent-learning impedance control for uncertain humans
  • Passivity-preserving learning; assistive and rehabilitation robotics

Area 2

Sim2Real gap

A formal proof that a controller synthesised on the mathematical model satisfies the original specification on the real system or simulator. Instead of folding the gap into the dynamics, we tighten the specification using δ-ISS bounds — keeping the model’s path optimality and temporal constraints. To our knowledge, the first treatment of the math-to-real gap through incremental stability.

  • Closeness conditions from incremental input-to-state stability
  • Specification tightening instead of conservative robust design
  • Hardware-in-the-loop validation of learning-enabled controllers

Area 3

Safe controller design for unknown systems

A unified learning-based framework that jointly synthesises the controller, a δ-ISS control Lyapunov function and a control barrier function with neural networks — then formally verifies them over compact sets. Complemented by prescribed performance control for constrained, uncertain and multi-agent systems.

  • Neural Lyapunov and barrier certificates with formal verification
  • Continuous-time, discrete-time, switched and cascaded (backstepping) systems
  • Prescribed performance and robust sliding-mode control under constraints

Area 4

Contraction theory and incremental stability

Rather than convergence to an equilibrium, incremental stability asks that any two trajectories converge to each other — a natural notion when the operating point keeps moving. I develop contraction-theoretic and differential-Lyapunov tools for non-smooth, switched, singularly perturbed and large-scale interconnected systems.

  • Switched systems with state-dependent switching; Zeno behaviour
  • δ-ISS / δ-ISpS, matrix measures and small-gain conditions for interconnections
  • 2-contraction for systems with multiple equilibria; observer-based design

Long-term vision

A unified theoretical and experimental framework for safe, learning-enabled autonomous systems — trajectory-based and energy-aware, built on incremental stability, contraction and passivity.

A

Learning-enabled safe control

Neural contraction metrics, safe adaptive and reinforcement learning, uncertainty-aware synthesis, learning-based observers, and verification of neural-network controllers.

B

Distributed and multi-agent systems

Contraction-based distributed control for large-scale networks: resilient coordination under failures and adversarial disturbances, and cooperative autonomy for heterogeneous robot teams.

C

Human-centric robotics

Passivity-preserving learning, adaptive impedance and admittance control, shared autonomy, and real-time safety guarantees for physical interaction.

D

Sim2Real stability and verification

Uncertainty-aware robustness analysis, hardware-in-the-loop validation, data-driven model refinement, and formal safety verification for learning-enabled systems.

Being set up

As a member of the Centre of Excellence for Robotics at VIT Vellore, I am setting up its robotics lab: a collaborative robot with force/torque sensing for human–robot collaboration tasks, multiple mobile robots and a 3D printer.
